PiggyPulse is a personal budget tracker that works the way you do — not on fixed monthly cycles, but on custom periods that match your real life.

Whether you get paid fortnightly, want to track spending per project, or simply prefer a different rhythm, PiggyPulse lets you define your own budget periods and track every pound, euro, or dollar against them.

TRACK YOUR SPENDING

Record income, expenses, and transfers between accounts  
Categorise transactions with custom categories and icons  
Assign merchants to track where your money goes  
Filter and search across all your transactions  

CUSTOM BUDGET PERIODS

Create periods of any length — weekly, fortnightly, monthly, or custom  
Set up auto-creation schedules so periods generate automatically  
Compare spending consistency across periods  

BUDGET PLANNING

Assign budgets to categories  
Track spending against limits with visual progress bars  
See projected spending pace and remaining budget at a glance  

MULTIPLE ACCOUNTS

Manage current accounts, savings, credit cards, and pocket money accounts  
View net position across all accounts  
Track balance changes per period  

PRIVACY FIRST

No bank connections — you control what data goes in  
No analytics or tracking  
No ads  
Your data is encrypted when stored  
Your data is stored in Europe  
Export or delete your data anytime  

BUILT FOR YOU

Dark themes designed for comfortable daily use  
Custom period selector accessible from every screen  
Pull-to-refresh and swipe actions throughout  
Face ID / biometric authentication  
Push notifications for budget alerts  

PiggyPulse helps you track your money manually, privately, and on your own schedule.
